## What a method is

A **pricing method** is the rule that turns costs into a selling price. Common methods include **cost plus** (costs plus a markup), **cost plus labor**, **per square foot** and **linear foot**. The methods you can choose depend on the product category.

## Reading an estimate

An estimate shows its parts so you can see why the price is what it is:

- material cost for the area or length,
- labor time at your hourly rate and burden,
- overhead,
- markup or margin,
- any minimum charge that applied.

## Tips

- Use a **minimum charge** for small jobs so setup time is covered.
- Compare methods for the same job when you are unsure; the calculator can show methods side by side for supported categories.
- A price you type over by hand needs a reason on the order item, and the reason is recorded.

> Every number in an estimate comes from your Pricing Foundation settings. If a price looks wrong, check [Materials, labor and equipment](/help/docs?article=materials-labor-equipment).
