Guided Onboarding is a checklist at **Help & Community > Onboarding**. It walks owners and admins through the setup that makes the rest of the app work well.

## How it works

- Each step has a status: not started, in progress, completed, skipped, deferred or blocked.
- **Required steps stay visible** until they are completed, even if you skip or defer them, so nothing important gets forgotten.
- Each step links to the screen where you do the work and to a help article that explains it.

## Who can use it

Owners and admins can complete setup steps. Staff can read progress.

## Tips

- Work top to bottom the first time; later steps depend on earlier ones.
- If a step is blocked, open its article to see what is missing.
- You can return later; progress is saved for your shop.
