SIGNGUY runs the day-to-day of a sign shop: customers, quotes, orders, production, invoices and payments, with pricing and reporting built on the same records.

## The recommended path

1. **Fill in your company profile.** Name, contact details and logo appear on quotes, invoices and the Customer Portal. See [Company profile](/help/docs?article=company-profile).
2. **Set up pricing.** Enter your labor rate, overhead and main materials so estimates are usable. See [Pricing setup](/help/docs?article=pricing-setup).
3. **Review your production workflow.** Confirm the stages your work moves through. See [Production workflow](/help/docs?article=production-workflow).
4. **Add your team.** Create employee records and decide who can do what. See [Employees and permissions](/help/docs?article=employees-and-permissions).
5. **Create a customer and a first order.** Running one real job end to end is the best test. See [Your first order](/help/docs?article=first-order).
