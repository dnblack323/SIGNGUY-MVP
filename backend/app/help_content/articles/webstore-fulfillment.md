## From buyer order to production

Buyers place orders on the live storefront. Totals are calculated by the system from the store's current public products; the buyer's browser cannot change prices.

Webstore orders can be turned into regular **orders** with their **order items**, so they flow through [production](/help/docs?article=production-workflow) like any other job. A buyer order is bridged once; repeating the action returns the same order.

## Fulfillment settings

Open **Webstore fulfillment settings** from the store to control how orders are handled. Review them before you launch.

## Orders & Production tab

The **Orders & Production** tab lists buyer orders and the production work created from them. Use **Messages & Activity** for a single history of what happened on the store.

## Fees and refunds

Platform fees are recorded as separate ledger entries. A refund creates reversing entries; original entries are never edited.
