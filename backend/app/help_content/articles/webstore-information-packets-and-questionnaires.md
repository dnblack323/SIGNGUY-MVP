## Information packet

The **information packet** tells the store owner how the process works and what you need from them. From the store header choose **Send Information Packet**.

## Questionnaire

The owner then fills in the **questionnaire**, which collects the store's name and branding details, who runs it, products and ordering rules. The Overview tab's Questionnaire card shows progress.

When answers arrive choose **Review Answers**. You can:

- accept the answers and continue setup, or
- send it back to the owner with **changes requested**. The store stage then shows that the owner has something to fix.

## Tips

- Send the packet as soon as the request is accepted; setup cannot move on until the questionnaire is answered.
- Use **Messages & Activity** to ask the owner a question instead of using email, so everything stays on the store.
