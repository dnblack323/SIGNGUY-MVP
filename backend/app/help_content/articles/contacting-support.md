## Which channel

- **Contact Support** is for questions, setup help and account problems. Choose the request type; the form shows where it will be routed.
- **Bug Reports** is for something that is broken or behaves wrongly.
- **Feature Requests** is for ideas. Other users can vote.

All three use the same support system, so there is one queue and one place to follow up.

## What to include

For bugs, say what you were doing, the steps to repeat it, what you expected and what happened. Add severity: low, medium, high or critical.

## Following up

Your requests list shows each one's **status**. When staff update a request or respond you get a notification in the app. Feature request and bug report responses appear on the item.

## Privacy and data requests

Use Contact Support and choose *Privacy or data request*.
