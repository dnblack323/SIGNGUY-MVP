Open **Control Center > Company Settings** (`/settings/company`).

## What to fill in

- **Business name, address, phone and email.** These appear on quotes, invoices and customer-facing pages.
- **Logo and brand details.** Used on documents and the Customer Portal.
- **Tax and payment preferences.** Set these before sending your first invoice.

## Why it matters

Documents pull from this page when they are created. Fix it before you send the first quote; changing it later does not rewrite documents that were already issued.

## After the profile

Continue with [Pricing setup](/help/docs?article=pricing-setup), then [Employees and permissions](/help/docs?article=employees-and-permissions).

> Online payment collection depends on your payment integration being connected in **Control Center > Integrations**. Until it is, record payments manually on invoices.
