There are two schedules, each with a different purpose.

## Shop Schedule

**Shop Operations > Schedule** shows operational appointments, order dates and production milestones from one shared calendar. Use it to plan installs and deliveries, reserve shop resources, and see what is due.

## Team Schedule

**Team & Productivity > Team Schedule** is where you build and publish a week's shifts for employees. Employees see published shifts in their Employee Portal.

## Tips

- Put install dates on the schedule so the team and the order stay in agreement.
- Publish the team schedule only when it is ready; employees see it as soon as it is published.
