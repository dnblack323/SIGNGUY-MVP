## I do not see a menu item or button

Your role may not have the permission, or the feature may not be enabled for your shop. Ask an owner or admin, or check **Control Center > Feature Access**.

## I cannot clock in

You need an employee record linked to your login. An owner or admin links it in Employees. See [Time Clock](/help/docs?article=time-clock).

## I cannot generate a work order

Only items marked as needing production are included. If none are, generation is refused. Mark the items that need production on the order.

## A job is not ready for production

Open the order's readiness check. It lists blockers such as a missing approval or a required deposit that has not been received.

## I cannot mark an invoice paid

Invoices become paid from recorded payments; you cannot set the status by hand. Use **Record payment**. See [Invoices and payments](/help/docs?article=invoices-and-payments).

## An expired quote will not convert

Expired quotes need an authorized override with a reason. Create a new revision or ask an owner or admin.

## A Webstore will not publish

Open **Approval & Launch** to see which gate is not met. See [Products, storefront and launch](/help/docs?article=webstore-products-and-launch).

## An AI tool shows a message instead of a result

The feature may not be enabled, or you may be out of credits. See [AI permissions, usage and limits](/help/docs?article=ai-boundaries).

## Still stuck

[Contact support](/help/docs?article=contacting-support) with what you were doing and what you saw.
