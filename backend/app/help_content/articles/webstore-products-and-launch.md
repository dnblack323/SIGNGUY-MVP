## Products

On the **Products** tab add products to the store. Each product has a basic description, pricing, options, images and production details, plus a review before it goes public. A product must be active, public and have a **selling price above zero** to be sold.

## Storefront

The **Storefront** tab holds the store's branding. Preview it before launch.

## Launch gates

A store can be published only when all of these are true:

- the Webstores add-on is active for your shop,
- the store is not closed or archived,
- at least one active public product has a positive price,
- the store has a public name and address,
- a launch packet exists,
- the owner has approved it, with terms and fees acknowledged,
- the payment setup for the store is ready.

The **Approval & Launch** tab shows each gate and why any are not met. When every gate passes the next action becomes **Publish Webstore**.

## After publishing

Customers can order only while the store is **Live** and checkout is enabled. You can **pause** a store, or **close** ordering when the campaign ends. Online checkout depends on your payment setup being ready; until it is, the store cannot take orders.
