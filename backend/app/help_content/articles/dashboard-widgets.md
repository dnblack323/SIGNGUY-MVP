**Home** is a dashboard of widgets: key numbers, summaries and short lists such as upcoming work and unpaid invoices.

- Which widgets you can use depends on your permissions.
- You can **hide**, **reorder** and **collapse** widgets to suit how you work.
- Widgets link to the related page so you can act on what you see.

If you see "Your account has no Home widgets available," ask an owner or admin to review your permissions.
