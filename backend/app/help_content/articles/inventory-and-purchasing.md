- **Inventory** tracks materials, stock levels, movements and locations. Reserved stock stays on hand; it is not physically removed until used.
- **Supply Center** searches the supplier catalog, compares warehouses and helps build purchase orders. Recommendations never mix incompatible material groups.
- **Purchase Orders** record what you ordered from each vendor. Vendors have their own records.

Material costs used in pricing are kept in [Pricing Foundation](/help/docs?article=materials-labor-equipment); inventory tracks quantities.
