## What is the difference between a quote and an order?

A quote is an offer to the customer. An order is the work you have agreed to do. Approved quotes convert to orders.

## Where did "jobs" go?

SIGNGUY uses **orders** and **work orders**. There is no separate job record.

## Why can I not edit an order total?

Totals are calculated from the items by the system. Change the items instead.

## If I change my prices, do old quotes change?

No. Quotes, orders and invoices keep the numbers they were created with. New estimates use the new settings.

## Can I delete an invoice?

You can void a draft or issued invoice with a reason, unless it has confirmed payments. Voiding keeps the record.

## Can customers pay online?

Only if online payment is connected and enabled for your shop. Otherwise record payments manually.

## Who can see pricing on a work order?

The printable work order summary leaves pricing off unless the viewer can see invoices.

## Does AI change my data on its own?

No. Proposed actions need your confirmation. See [AI permissions, usage and limits](/help/docs?article=ai-boundaries).
