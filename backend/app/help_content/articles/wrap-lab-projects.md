**Shop Operations > Wrap Lab** manages vehicle wrap projects from the commercial record through inspection, proofing, production, installation and aftercare. Wrap Lab is included with your core plan.

## Create a project

1. Select to create a project and choose the customer.
2. Record the **vehicle** (**Vehicle & Specs** tab): make, model and details used for measurements.
3. Plan **coverage** and record **measurements** for each area to wrap.
4. Build the **estimate** and link a **quote**.

## Inspection

Record the vehicle's condition on the **Inspection** tab before work starts, so existing damage is on record.

## Artwork and proofs

Use **Proofs & Files** for design and proofing. Original logos keep their source file; silent redrawing or font substitution is not allowed, and locked layers cannot be edited until someone authorized unlocks them. When artwork is final, get a [proof approved](/help/docs?article=approvals-and-proofs).

## Stages

A project moves through stages one step at a time, requiring the right permission to advance: from lead intake to vehicle recorded, measurement planning, estimate ready, quote linked, contract and deposit, pre-install ready, design in progress, proof ready and approved, and onward to production. See [production and installation](/help/docs?article=wrap-lab-production-and-installation).

> Automatic vehicle lookup and AI-generated wrap design are not part of Wrap Lab today.
