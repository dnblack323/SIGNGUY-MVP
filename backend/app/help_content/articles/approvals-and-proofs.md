**Shop Operations > Approval Center** is the place to see approvals that are waiting, approved or have changes requested.

## How approvals work

1. Attach artwork or a proof to the order or quote.
2. Send it for approval. Customers review proofs in the [Customer Portal](/help/docs?article=customer-portal).
3. The customer approves or asks for changes. Their decision is recorded on the order.

An order's readiness check flags items whose approval is missing or has changes requested, so unapproved work is visible before it reaches production.

## Decision Rooms

A **Decision Room** presents comparison options to a customer so they can choose. Authoring is in **Decision Rooms**; the customer sees the room through their portal.

## Tips

- Keep each approval tied to the exact version of the artwork the customer saw.
- When changes are requested, upload the new version and send it again rather than editing the old one.
