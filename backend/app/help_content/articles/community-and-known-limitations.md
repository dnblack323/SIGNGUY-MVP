## Available today

- **Community** posts in an existing space, with voting.
- **Founders** spaces are readable by people who have been granted access.
- **Bug Reports**, **Feature Requests** (with voting) and **Contact Support** work as described in [Contacting support](/help/docs?article=contacting-support).

## Not available yet

- Opening a post to read or write comments (comment counts are shown).
- Creating or managing Community spaces from the app.
- Editing posts, reporting posts, and moderation screens.
- Founder access administration and a Founders posting experience.
- Attachments and duplicate handling on bug reports from the app.
- An article editor for Help content. Documentation is managed by the platform.

These are planned as separate work and are not required to use the app day to day.
