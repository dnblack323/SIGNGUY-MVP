## Permissions

Owners and admins control AI through **Control Center > AI Governance** and **Feature Access**. Only people with AI permission see the tools.

## Credits

AI is included with your plan and limited by **credits**. **AI Credits** shows your balance, the ledger of what used credits, and alerts.

## What AI will not do

- It never changes billing, invoices or payments.
- It does not act without your confirmation when it proposes a change.
- It is metered and logged in **AI Activity**.

## Availability

Which AI features work in your shop depends on your plan, your credits and platform configuration. A feature that is not enabled shows a message rather than a result. AI results can be wrong; check them before use.
