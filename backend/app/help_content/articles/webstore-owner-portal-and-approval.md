## The Owner Portal

Store owners and managers sign in to the portal at `/portal` and open their Webstore. They can:

- complete the questionnaire,
- upload files and branding,
- review products,
- review the launch packet and approve it,
- exchange messages with your shop.

Owners are invited by email and can only see their own store.

## Approval

Before launch the owner must review the **launch packet**, accept the terms and fee acknowledgement, and approve the store. Create the packet with **Create Launch Packet** and deliver it with **Send Launch Packet**. The owner's approval moves the store to the next stage.

## If the owner cannot be reached

The store header tells you when the owner cannot receive messages, for example because their email is missing. Fix the contact details on the store before continuing.
