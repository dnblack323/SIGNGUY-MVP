## Two views of the same work

- **Production Board** (`/work-orders/board`) shows active work orders as a live queue by stage. Priority and due date set the order within a column, and overdue jobs are flagged.
- **Work Orders** (`/work-orders`) is the production floor list, with filters for status, order and customer.

Moving a card on the board uses the same rules as changing status on the work order, so the two never disagree.

## Assigning work

Open a work order and assign it to one or more people in your shop. People from another shop cannot be assigned. New assignees are notified, and you can filter the board by assignee.

## The work order summary

A printable **summary** gives the shop floor the specifications they need. Pricing is left off unless the person viewing has permission to see invoices.

## Production kiosk

`/kiosk/production` is a full-screen view for a shop-floor display.

## Tips

- Use priority *rush* sparingly so it stays meaningful.
- Add a reason whenever you block a job.
