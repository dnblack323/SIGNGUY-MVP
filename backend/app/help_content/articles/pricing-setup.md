Good estimates need your own costs. This is the order we recommend.

## 1. Run Quick Setup

Open **Business & Finance > Pricing Defaults** and choose **Quick Setup (Grouped Quiz)**. The quiz asks what you normally charge for common jobs and what you charge at minimum. It then **suggests** values.

Suggestions are provisional. Nothing changes until an owner or admin **approves** the fields they want to apply.

## 2. Check labor and overhead

Review your hourly labor rates, labor burden and default overhead. See [Materials, labor and equipment](/help/docs?article=materials-labor-equipment).

## 3. Add your main materials

Add the materials you buy most (vinyl, banner material, substrates) with their costs so calculators have real numbers.

## 4. Test with a real job

Open the [calculator](/help/docs?article=pricing-calculators) for a product you sell, enter a job you have priced before, and compare. Adjust until it matches how you want to price.

## Good to know

- Starter defaults are placeholders to get you moving; replace them with your own costs.
- Changing defaults affects new estimates only. Saved quotes, orders and invoices keep the numbers they were created with.
