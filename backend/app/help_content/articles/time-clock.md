## Time Clock

At **Team & Productivity > Time Clock** an employee can **clock in**, take **breaks** and **clock out**. Your account must be linked to an employee record first; owners and admins do the linking in [Employees](/help/docs?article=employees-and-permissions).

## Timesheets

**Timesheets** shows daily, weekly and monthly time totals built from clock entries.

## Payroll

**Payroll** is an internal gross-pay ledger: hours, advances, payments and carryover. It is a record-keeping aid. It is not a tax or filing service and does not file payroll taxes.

## Troubleshooting

- *No employee record linked:* ask an owner or admin to link your login.
- *Hours look wrong:* review the entries on Timesheets with someone who has permission to manage them.
