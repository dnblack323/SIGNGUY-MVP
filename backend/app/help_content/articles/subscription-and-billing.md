**Control Center > Subscriptions** shows your plan, trial, setup purchases and access state. It is for owners and admins. **Feature Access** lists what is enabled for your shop, and **AI Credits** shows balances and usage.

## If a payment fails

The Help Center reads your current billing state and shows guidance. It never changes billing.

- **Recently past due:** update billing details or retry payment.
- **Longer past due:** an owner or admin should resolve billing to avoid further restrictions.
- **Eligible for suspension or suspended:** correct billing details first; if they are already correct, contact support.

Fix billing from **Subscriptions**, which is the source of truth.
