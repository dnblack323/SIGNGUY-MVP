Owners and admins manage the shop itself.

- **Setup:** [Company profile](/help/docs?article=company-profile), [Pricing setup](/help/docs?article=pricing-setup) and [Guided Onboarding](/help/docs?article=guided-onboarding).
- **People:** add employees and set roles. See [Employees and permissions](/help/docs?article=employees-and-permissions).
- **Money:** approve pricing defaults, review [finances](/help/docs?article=finances-and-expenses) and [reports](/help/docs?article=reports).
- **Plan and AI:** [Subscription and billing](/help/docs?article=subscription-and-billing) and [AI permissions](/help/docs?article=ai-boundaries).
- **Settings:** everything in [Control Center](/help/docs?article=control-center-settings).
