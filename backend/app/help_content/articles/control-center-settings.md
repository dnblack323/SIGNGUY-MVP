**Control Center** is for owners and admins.

- **Company Settings:** business profile and branding. See [Company profile](/help/docs?article=company-profile).
- **Integrations:** third-party services connected to your shop. Secrets are never shown here.
- **Production Workflows:** the stage definitions your work moves through. See [Production workflow](/help/docs?article=production-workflow).
- **Subscriptions:** your plan, trials and access state. See [Subscription and billing](/help/docs?article=subscription-and-billing).
- **AI Credits:** balances and usage for AI tools.
- **Feature Access:** which features your shop has enabled.
- **AI Governance:** shop-level rules for AI use.
- **Data & Security:** audit and data-handling information. See [Privacy and data deletion](/help/docs?article=privacy-data-deletion).
