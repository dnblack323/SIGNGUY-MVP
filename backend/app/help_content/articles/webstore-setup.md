A **Webstore** is an online store you run for a customer, such as a team, school or business. The **store owner** supplies the details and approves the store; you set it up, publish it and fulfill the orders.

> Webstores is an add-on. If you do not see it in navigation, check **Control Center > Subscriptions** and **Feature Access**.

## The journey

Every store has one **stage** that all screens agree on:

1. **Request received:** you start a request from an intake or from Webstores.
2. **Waiting on owner:** the owner completes the information packet and questionnaire.
3. **Setting up:** you review their answers and build the store.
4. **Owner approval:** the owner reviews and approves the store.
5. **Payment setup:** payment setup is finished.
6. **Ready to publish:** all launch checks pass.
7. **Live**, then **Paused** or **Closed** when ordering ends. Stores can be **Archived**.

## One next action

The store header always shows **one next action** and who owns it, you or the owner. Use its button instead of hunting through tabs. When the owner is the one to act, the button opens the right tab so you can see the status.
