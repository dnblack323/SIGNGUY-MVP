- **Tasks** are shared to-dos that can link back to a customer, order or other record. Staff see their own work views.
- **Messages** are conversations between team members.
- **Announcements** are shop-wide notices that everyone sees.

Notifications appear in the app when you are assigned work or a record you follow changes.
