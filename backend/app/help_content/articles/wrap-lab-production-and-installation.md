## Production

After the proof is approved, the project reaches **panel plan ready** and **production ready**. The **panel plan** divides the design into printable panels. The **Production** tab tracks the work.

## Installation

Schedule the install on the shop schedule; the project moves to **install scheduled** and then **installing**. Use **Install & QC** to record the installation and the quality check.

## Completion

When installation is finished a **completion packet** is generated. Packets are saved snapshots; generating one again creates a new revision instead of changing the old one. The project is then **completed**, and a **warranty** can be recorded to start **warranty active**. Finished projects can be archived, and archived projects are read-only.

## Money

Wrap Lab amounts are stored in whole cents and tied to the project's quote and invoice records.
