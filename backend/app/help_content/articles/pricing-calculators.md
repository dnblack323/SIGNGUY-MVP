The **Pricing Calculator** (`/pricing-calculator`) is a workspace for pricing a job by product category. Pick a category, enter size, material and options, choose a method where several are offered, and the estimate updates.

## Using the result

- Add the priced item to a **quote** or **order**.
- **Save** a calculation to reuse it later from the saved calculation library.

## Availability

Calculators are available for the categories configured for your shop. Some categories are more complete than others; if a category you sell is missing or limited, price it as a manual line item and tell us through [Feature requests](/help/docs?article=contacting-support).

## Tips

- Check [Pricing setup](/help/docs?article=pricing-setup) first; a calculator is only as good as your costs.
- Totals shown on quotes and orders are recalculated by the system, not by the browser.
