What you see depends on the permissions your owner has given you.

- **Start of day:** clock in on the [Time Clock](/help/docs?article=time-clock) and check your [tasks](/help/docs?article=tasks-messages-and-announcements).
- **Work:** find your jobs on the [Production Board](/help/docs?article=production-board).
- **Customers and orders:** see [Customers](/help/docs?article=customers) and [Orders](/help/docs?article=orders).
- **Stuck?** Search this Documentation, or see [Troubleshooting](/help/docs?article=troubleshooting).
