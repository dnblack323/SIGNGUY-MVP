## Employee records and logins

An **employee record** describes a person: contact details, pay information and work history. A **login** lets them sign in. Link the two so a person can clock in and see their own work. If someone sees "No employee record linked" on the Time Clock, an owner or admin needs to link their account.

Add people at **Team & Productivity > Employees**. The **Employee Portal** page lets you invite active employees to a self-service portal for their schedule, time clock and timesheet.

## Roles

SIGNGUY has three shop roles:

- **Owner** and **Admin** can manage the whole shop, including settings, pricing, billing and users.
- **Staff** can work in customers, quotes, orders, production, tasks and documents according to the permissions granted to that role.

Navigation items and buttons are hidden or disabled when you lack the permission. The server enforces permissions too, so hiding is never the only protection.

## Good practice

- Give most people the Staff role and promote only the people who manage settings and money.
- Deactivate employees who leave instead of deleting them, so history stays intact.
- Check **Control Center > Feature Access** to see which features your shop has enabled.
