**Templates** (`/templates`) are reusable pieces of text for intake, Decision Rooms, productivity, communication, reminders and support responses.

## Placeholders

A placeholder is a token such as a customer name that is replaced when the template is used. Only approved placeholders can be used; the editor previews missing values before you save so a template never goes out with a blank.

## Tips

- Keep templates short and specific.
- Preview with a real record before you rely on a template.
