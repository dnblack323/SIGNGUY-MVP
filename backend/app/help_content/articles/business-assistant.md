The **Business Assistant** (**Tools & Resources > Business Assistant**) is a chat assistant for your shop. AI credits apply to its use.

## What it can do

- Answer questions and draft text such as emails.
- Propose **actions** from what you ask, for example creating a record.
- Keep **memory** notes you choose to save, and run **routines** you set up.
- Show **insights** about your business that you can dismiss.
- Hand design and writing requests to the Studio tools. See [AI Studio tools](/help/docs?article=ai-studio-tools).

## It asks before it acts

When the assistant wants to change something it creates a **proposal** that you can edit, confirm or cancel. Nothing is executed until you confirm. It cannot move money, change invoices or payments, or change Webstore payouts.

## Permissions and limits

- You need permission to use the assistant, and it can only reach records you are allowed to see.
- Use is metered in credits. See [AI permissions, usage and limits](/help/docs?article=ai-boundaries).

> Availability of live AI providers and voice features depends on how your shop and the platform are configured. If a feature is not enabled you will see a message in place of a result.
