## Materials

Material costs are kept in Pricing Foundation under **Materials & Pricing Profiles**. Keep them current when your supplier prices change. Stock levels are tracked separately in [Inventory](/help/docs?article=inventory-and-purchasing).

## Labor

Hourly rates for production and installation labor (including helper and specialty technician rates) and **labor burden** (a percentage for taxes and benefits) are set in Pricing Foundation. Include burden, or labor will be under-priced.

## Overhead

**Default overhead** is a percentage that spreads the cost of running the shop across jobs. Raise it if you are not covering rent, utilities and software.

## Equipment

Some services add equipment costs, such as a lift needed for an install, with a daily rate. Record the equipment you own at **Team & Productivity > Equipment**.

## What changes when you edit

New estimates use the new values. Existing quotes, orders and invoices do not change.
