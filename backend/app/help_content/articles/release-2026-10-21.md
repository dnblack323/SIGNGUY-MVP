- **One stage, one next action.** Every Webstore has one stage that staff screens and the owner portal agree on, and the header shows the one thing to do next and who owns it.
- **Regrouped workspace.** The store workspace is organized into Overview, Setup, Products, Storefront, Approval & Launch, Orders & Production, and Messages & Activity.
- **Messages per store.** Shop and store owner messages live on the store, next to a single activity history.
- **Safer setup tasks.** Tasks generated by the setup flow are not duplicated, and returned questionnaires clearly show that changes were requested.
