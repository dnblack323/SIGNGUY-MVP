## Scheduling an install

Put the install on the **Shop Schedule** so the crew, the order and the customer agree on the date. See [Scheduling](/help/docs?article=scheduling).

## Completion readiness

The order's **Completion readiness** section lists what still stands between the job and done. Use the **Completion & Comms** tab for customer messages about delivery or pickup, and the *Issues and rework* section to record a problem instead of reopening the order.

## Closing out

1. Finish the work order stages so the work order is completed.
2. Confirm the invoice is created and paid; record any remaining payment.
3. Mark the order completed.

Completed orders are protected against accidental financial changes.

Vehicle wrap installs have their own checklist. See [Wrap Lab production and installation](/help/docs?article=wrap-lab-production-and-installation).
