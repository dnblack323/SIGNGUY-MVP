**Business & Finance > Reports** offers:

- **Business and finance reports** you can run straight away.
- A **custom report builder** for the fields and filters you choose.
- **Saved reports** you can reopen.
- **Scheduled runs** that produce a report on a schedule.
- **Approved exports** of report data.

What you can see and export depends on your permissions. Reports use the same records as the rest of the app, so numbers match the invoices, orders and payments they come from.

## Tips

- Check the date range and basis shown on a report before you share it.
- Save a report once you have it right so you do not rebuild it.
