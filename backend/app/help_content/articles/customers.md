**Shop Operations > Customers** lists everyone you have done work for. Select **New customer** to add one, and search the list to find existing customers.

## The customer record

Open a customer to see tabs for:

- **Overview** and **Customer profile**
- **Contacts** and **Addresses** (add several of each)
- **Quotes**, **Orders**, **Invoices** and **Work orders** for this customer
- **Files & Forms** and **Communications**
- **Requests** (intake and Webstore requests)
- **Portal** access for the Customer Portal
- **Activity**

Because every quote, order and invoice links to the customer, the record shows the whole relationship in one place.

## Duplicates

If the same customer was entered twice, use **Duplicates** to review likely matches and **Merge into survivor** to combine them. The surviving record keeps the history.

## Tips

- Enter an email address if you want the customer to use the [Customer Portal](/help/docs?article=customer-portal).
- Add a contact for each person you deal with at a company.
