## Finance overview

**Business & Finance > Overview** shows your finance dashboard. Totals are labeled by basis: **cash received** and **invoice basis** are shown side by side and never silently mixed, so read the label before comparing numbers.

## Expenses

**Expenses** records operational shop spending. Amounts are stored in cents. Renaming an expense category never rewrites the labels on past rows.

## Taxes

**Taxes** summarizes sales tax from the tax values saved on each invoice. Changing your current tax settings does not rewrite historical invoices. These are operational summaries and are not filing advice.
