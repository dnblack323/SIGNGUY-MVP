**Sales > Intake** holds requests captured before they become a quote or an order.

## Create an intake

1. Select **New Intake**.
2. Choose or add the customer and describe what they want.
3. **Add item** for each thing requested, then **Save**.

## What happens next

From the intake you move the work forward: create a [quote](/help/docs?article=quotes) or an [order](/help/docs?article=orders). If the request is for a store, use **Start Webstore request**, then **Open Webstore** once it exists. See [Webstore setup](/help/docs?article=webstore-setup).

Intake keeps early requests out of your order list until they are real work.
