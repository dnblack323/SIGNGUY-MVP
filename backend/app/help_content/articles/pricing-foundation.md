**Business & Finance > Pricing Defaults** opens **Pricing Foundation**, the one place your pricing inputs live. Every calculator and estimate reads from it.

## Sections

- **Shop & Categories:** shop-wide defaults such as hourly rates, labor burden and default overhead, plus per-category setup. Category wizards help you fill in each product type.
- **Materials & Pricing Profiles:** material costs and the pricing profiles used for them.
- **Pricing Components:** reusable cost components that estimates can include.
- **Saved / Common Items:** items you price often, saved for reuse.

## Who can change it

Anyone with pricing permission can view it; changing defaults needs write permission, normally owners and admins.
