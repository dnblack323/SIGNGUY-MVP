**Sales > Quotes** is where you create, price, send and track customer quotes.

## Workflow

1. **New quote**, then choose the customer.
2. Add line items. Prices can come from the [pricing calculators](/help/docs?article=pricing-calculators).
3. Use **Customer-safe preview** to see exactly what the customer will see.
4. **Email quote**, or share it through the Customer Portal.
5. The customer approves or declines. You can also record the decision.
6. **Convert to order** once approved.

## Statuses

Draft, sent, viewed, approved, declined, expired, converted and void.

- A quote's **expiry** is calculated when you open it. An expired quote cannot be converted unless an authorized person gives a reason; that override is recorded.
- A converted quote is read-only. A void quote cannot be edited or converted.

## Revisions

Once a quote is sent, changing customer-visible details creates a new **revision** and keeps the old one. Open **Revision history** to see what the customer saw at each point. Totals are always calculated by the system from the current revision.

## Estimates

An estimate is a calculated price, not a separate document. See [Pricing methods and estimates](/help/docs?article=pricing-methods).
