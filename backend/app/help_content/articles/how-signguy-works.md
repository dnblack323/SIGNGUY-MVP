## The main areas

The left navigation groups the app into areas:

- **Home** is your dashboard of widgets. See [Dashboard widgets](/help/docs?article=dashboard-widgets).
- **Shop Operations** holds Customers, Sales (intake, quotes, orders), Approval Center, Production, Schedule, Webstores and Wrap Lab.
- **Business & Finance** holds Invoices, Expenses, Taxes, Reports and Pricing Defaults.
- **Team & Productivity** holds Employees, Equipment, Training, Tasks, Time Clock, Timesheets, Payroll and messaging.
- **Tools & Resources** holds the Business Assistant and the Studio AI tools.
- **Control Center** holds settings, subscription, integrations and data security.
- **Help & Community** holds this documentation, Onboarding, support and What's New.

You only see the areas your role is allowed to use.

## How a job flows

1. A **customer** record exists, or is created.
2. A request arrives as an **intake**, or you start a **quote**. Quotes can be sent and approved.
3. An approved quote converts to an **order**, or you create an order directly. The order holds the **order items** and their prices.
4. Items that need production become a **Work Order**, which moves through your **production stages** on the Production Board.
5. An **invoice** is created from the order, and **payments** are recorded against it.

Each step reuses the same customer and order records, so there is nothing to retype and reports always agree with the work.

## Records worth knowing

- **Order** is the record for a customer's job. SIGNGUY does not use a separate "job" record.
- **Work Order** is the production copy of an order's production items.
- **Invoice** has a document status (draft, issued, void) and a separate financial status (unpaid, partial, paid). Order status never says "paid".
