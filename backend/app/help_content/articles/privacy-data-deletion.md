Your shop's data is kept separate from every other shop. **Control Center > Data & Security** shows audit information and data-handling details for owners and admins.

## Requests

For a privacy or data request, such as export or deletion of records, use **Contact Support** and choose *Privacy or data request*. See [Contacting support](/help/docs?article=contacting-support).

## Good practice

- Archive records instead of deleting them when you may need the history.
- Use the audit trail to see who changed what.
