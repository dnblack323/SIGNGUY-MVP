- **Documents** is the shared file library. Files are private by default and every download is authenticated.
- **Forms** is the form builder library. Forms are used today to collect Webstore questionnaires; other uses may follow.
- **Email History** lists emails sent from the app so you can confirm what a customer received.

For job artwork see [Artwork and files](/help/docs?article=artwork-and-files).
