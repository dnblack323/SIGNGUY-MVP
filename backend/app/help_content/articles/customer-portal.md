The **Customer Portal** is a separate sign-in for your customers, at `/portal`.

## What customers can do

- View **quotes** and approve or decline them
- View **orders** and their progress
- View **invoices** and pay them when online payment is available
- Review **proofs** and take part in **Decision Rooms**
- Download **documents** and send **messages**
- Manage their **profile**
- Open any **Webstores** they own

Customers sign in at `/portal/login` with their email and either a password or an emailed sign-in link.

## Access on the customer record

The **Portal** tab on a customer shows the portal sign-ins that exist for that customer, with their status.

> **Not available yet:** the customer record does not yet have an "Invite to portal" button. Portal sign-ins are created for you today; if a customer needs access and has none listed, use [Contact Support](/help/docs?article=contacting-support) and name the customer.

## Staff and portal accounts are separate

A customer sign-in cannot open the staff app, and staff sign-ins are not used in the portal.

## Troubleshooting

If a customer cannot sign in, check the email address on their record and the status shown on the Portal tab, then ask them to use the emailed sign-in link.
