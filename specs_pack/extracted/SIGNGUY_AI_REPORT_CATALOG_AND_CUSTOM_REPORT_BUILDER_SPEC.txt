Internal Product Specification 
SignGuy AI Report Catalog 
Report Categories, Standard Reports, Builder Rules, and Export Structure 
Purpose: Create one reporting system that can analyze data across the entire SignGuy AI platform without creating 
duplicate report engines in Operations, Business Management, Webstores, Wrap Lab, or Team tools. 
Permanent Location in App: Business Management → Reports 
Operational modules may open filtered report shortcuts, but they all use the same shared Reports engine and the same report 
definitions. 
1. Reports Navigation Structure 
Report Area Purpose 
Overview High-level business and shop performance dashboard. 
Financial Reports Revenue, billing, payments, profitability, taxes, and cash-related 
reporting. 
Operations Reports Orders, production, approvals, documents, and service delivery 
performance. 
Customer & Sales Reports Customers, quotes, conversion, repeat business, and account 
performance. 
Webstore Reports Store sales, owner payouts, products, fundraising, and platform 
performance. 
Materials & Purchasing Reports Inventory, vendor spending, purchase orders, material usage, 
and waste. 
Team & Labor Reports Hours, payroll, labor efficiency, workload, attendance, and 
scheduling. 
Wrap Lab Reports Wrap project performance, install labor, vehicle mix, material 
use, and service quality. 
Custom Reports Build, save, share, and reuse reports across any approved data 
source. 
Scheduled Reports Automated recurring delivery of saved reports. 
Exports Central location for CSV, Excel, PDF, accounting, tax, payroll, 
and data exports. 
2. Standard Report Page Components 
 Date range selector: today, this week, last week, month-to-date, quarter-to-date, year-to-date, custom dates, and 
comparison period. 
 Saved filters: customer, service/category, order type, salesperson, employee, location, store, production stage, payment 
status, vendor, and custom tags. 
 Summary cards: the most important totals, changes from the comparison period, and exception counts. 
 Charts: only when they help detect a trend, bottleneck, or comparison. Tables remain available for actual numbers.

11. Custom Reports 
Custom Reports is the build-anything workspace. It is not a second reporting system; it uses the same approved data, filters, totals, and 
drill-down behavior as all standard reports. 
Core Builder Flow 
 Choose a primary data source. 
 Choose columns and calculated fields. 
 Add filters and date range rules. 
 Group, sort, subtotal, and compare data. 
 Choose table, chart, summary-card, or combined layout. 
 Save as a private report, shared team report, or role-restricted report. 
 Schedule delivery or export on demand.

14. Architecture Rules to Protect 
 One reporting engine only. Operations, Webstores, Wrap Lab, Materials, Payroll, and Team pages may provide filtered 
shortcuts, but they never create separate report builders. 
 Reports must preserve source-of-truth links. Every total should drill into the underlying records when the user has 
permission. 
 Role permissions apply to both viewing and exporting. Payroll, cost, margin, and platform-level data must be protected. 
 Every saved report stores its data source, fields, filters, groupings, calculation definitions, visualization, owner, 
permissions, and schedule as one report definition. 
 Custom calculated fields must be clearly labeled and must not overwrite stored financial or operational values. 
 Use estimated versus actual comparisons wherever the app has both values, especially labor, materials, production 
duration, and profitability. 
 Keep default reports opinionated and practical. The builder is for flexibility, not an excuse to make every user invent basic 
shop reports from scratch.
