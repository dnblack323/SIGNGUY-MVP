# Master Order for Completing the Remaining Sections

Do not work on every section at the same time. Complete one section through final review before opening a large correction batch in the next section.

## Recommended order

1. **Business & Finance**
   - It is directly connected to completed Shop Operations records such as Orders, Invoices, Payments, Webstore transactions, and financial status.
   - It also forces the Pricing Defaults placement correction into Control Center.

2. **Control Center**
   - Complete Pricing Foundation placement, company settings, permissions, integrations, subscriptions, AI credits, feature access, and security.
   - Other areas rely on these controls.

3. **Team & Productivity**
   - Complete employees, schedules, tasks, timekeeping, payroll, communication, training, equipment, and the Employee Portal.
   - Connect it to the Shop Schedule and Business & Finance payroll-cost reporting.

4. **Tools & Resources**
   - Complete Library, Templates, AI tools, Business Assistant, prompts, generated assets, provider behavior, and credit enforcement after Control Center is stable.

5. **Help & Community**
   - Update documentation and onboarding after the main navigation and workflows are stable.
   - Complete community, bugs, feature requests, support, and release-note workflows.

## Repeat these steps for each section

1. Confirm what belongs in the section.
2. Correct the main module navigation.
3. Decide internal tabs and contextual ribbons.
4. Update the code-based feature audit.
5. Create or correct the numbered gap list.
6. Recheck every gap against the actual code.
7. Group real gaps into dependency-based implementation batches.
8. Complete workflows from the interface through the backend.
9. Fix cross-section links without duplicating records.
10. Check permissions, tenant separation, privacy, and audit history.
11. Clean up confusing interfaces.
12. Refactor oversized or duplicated code.
13. Add focused and regression tests.
14. Run backend tests, frontend tests, and the frontend build.
15. Update the category register and closure evidence.
16. Perform the final category-specific code review.
17. Update the overall code issue register.
18. Mark the section complete in Donnell's owner documents.

## Completion rule

A section is not complete just because its pages exist. It is complete only when its placement, navigation, workflows, permissions, tenant separation, history, tests, registers, and final code review are all finished or any remaining limitations are clearly recorded.
